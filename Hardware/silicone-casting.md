# Silicone Casting
This is a general guide on how to work with silicone and cast the standard robot. 

More detailed instructions for **preparing other molds** are available in the cloud:
- [`RR` - Retractable Robot](www.todo.de)

## Chose the silicone type
|  |  | ZA 00 Translucid | RTV Silikon ZA 22 Mould | RTV Silikon HT 33 Transparent | RTV Silikon HT 45 Transparent |
|--|--|:----------------:|:-----------------------:|:-----------------------------:|:-----------------------------:|
| **Density [kg/l]** | **Dichte [kg/l]** | 1.03 | 1.13 | 1.09 | 1.11 |
| **Processing Time [min]** | **Bearbeitungszeit [min]** | 10 | 15 | 20 | 12|
| **Curing Time [h]** | **Aushärtezeit [h]** | 1 | 1.5 | 3.5 | 2.5|
| **Shore Hardness [ShA]** | **Shore Härte [ShA]** | 0 | 22 | 33 | 43|
| **Tensile Strength [N/mm²]** | **Zugfestigkeit [N/mm²]** | 1.1 | 4.5 | 1.2 | 5.0|
| **Elongation at Break [%]** | **Bruchdehnung [%]** | 400 | 480 | 460 | 250|

Most of tendon based robots are made of `RTV Silikon HT 33 Transparent` but a few (mostly older versions) are made of `RTV Silikon HT 45 Transparent`

The higher the Shore hardness, the harder the silicone.

## Prepare the mold

The silicone releases easily from the 3D-printed molds. Therefore, no further processing or coating of the mold is necessary.
