### Step 2 Put in the other parts
<img src="Images_molds/RT6_step5.png" alt="step 5" width="200" align="right">
Put the spring steal wire into the tubes to make them stay straight.

Then put everything together. The imu circuit boards should go into the middle of the mounting points(green).

Make sure that the wires stay in the middle of the mold by taping it to the thick tube.

<br clear="right">

> [!IMPORTANT]
> You cant replace cast-in parts after the silicone is cured. Therefore make sure that the circuit boards and imus are working before starting to work with silicone.

## Casting
To make the mold stand upright you can use a construction like this:

<img src="Images_molds/RT6_real5.jpg" alt="foto 5: set up the mold" width="200">

Now follow the instructions on [working with silicone](../silicone-casting.md)

## Remove the mold
1. Pull out the spring steal wire
2. Cut the tubes (use a cutter knife and cut them through the slots in the mold)
3. Remove the mold (a screwdriver might be useful)
